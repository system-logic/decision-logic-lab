# Decision Logic Lab Manifesto

## Scope
A research log of architectural and decision-making thinking in real-world contexts.  
Not education, not prescription, not generalization.

## What This Is
- Public record of reasoning in specific situations  
- Demonstration of how decisions form, break, and get revised  
- Archive of structures, boundaries, constraints

## What This Is Not
- Tutorial, course, best practices, templates, advice  

## Responsibility Boundary
Understanding a structure is not the same as a reason to apply it.  
Every example is valid only in its original context; carrying it elsewhere is the reader's own decision.

## Tone
No universal claims. No "one should". No better/worse comparisons.

## Desired Takeaway
"I understand the structure, and I understand that applying it is a decision I must make and answer for myself."
